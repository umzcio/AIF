# UM_Standards — Administrative Staff

Reference file for administrative staff in HR, finance, procurement, legal, compliance,
and executive support roles at the University of Montana. Load this file when the user's
context involves employee records, financial data, contracts, or institutional compliance.

---

## Employee Data & HIPAA

Administrative staff frequently handle data protected by HIPAA, state employment law,
and UM HR policy.

### What You Cannot Enter Into Claude

- Employee names combined with: salary, medical/leave status, disciplinary history,
  accommodation details, or performance evaluation specifics
- Health plan, benefits, or FMLA documentation tied to named individuals
- Workers' compensation claim details
- Immigration or I-9 documentation
- Candidate information from active hiring processes (names, application materials)

### What You Can Do

- Draft HR policy documents, templates, and communications using anonymized examples
- Develop job descriptions, position profiles, and onboarding materials
- Create training content using fictional scenarios
- Summarize or analyze aggregate workforce data (headcount, turnover rates) without
  individual identification

**Rule of thumb**: If a colleague could identify themselves or another employee from
the data you're sharing, remove identifying details before using Claude.

## Finance & Procurement

### Sensitive Financial Data

- Do not share budget documents containing named individual salaries
- Vendor pricing, contract terms, and negotiation positions are confidential —
  share only at the level of detail needed for the specific task
- Grant financial reports: remove named personnel salary detail before using Claude
- Audit findings and management letters: treat as confidential; human review required
  before any AI-assisted response

### Safe Uses

- Drafting budget narratives and financial justifications (with anonymized personnel data)
- Developing procurement specifications and RFP language
- Creating financial policy documentation and training materials
- Summarizing budget categories and program costs at an aggregate level

---

## Contracts & Legal

- Do not share draft or executed contracts containing confidential terms with Claude
  without legal review of your AI use policy
- Attorney-client privileged communications must never be entered into Claude
- Settlement agreements, litigation strategy, and legal opinions: off-limits
- For contract template development (non-confidential boilerplate): Claude is appropriate
  with human legal review before finalization

---

## Compliance & Regulatory

### Title IX, ADA, and Employee Relations

- Active investigation files, complaints, and case notes: do not use Claude
- Policy development and training materials: appropriate use with review
- Reporting templates and process documentation: appropriate use

### Public Records and FOIA

- Assume anything you enter into Claude could be subject to public records request
- Do not use Claude to strategize around records requests or litigation holds
- Communications about pending or anticipated litigation: consult legal counsel

## AI Disclosure for Administrative Contexts

- **External communications** (to students, vendors, accreditors, state/federal agencies):
  Disclose AI assistance; human signature and review required
- **Board of Regents materials**: Human authorship and review required; disclose AI assistance
- **Internal policy documents**: Disclosure recommended; legal/compliance review before publication
- **Routine correspondence and templates**: Disclosure optional for internal use

## Accessibility — Administrative Contexts (WCAG 2.1 AA)

Administrative staff produce communications and documents that reach broad audiences —
students, employees, vendors, and the public. Accessibility in these materials is a
legal obligation under the ADA and Section 504.

### Documents and Forms
- **Word and PDF**: Use heading styles, not manual bold/size formatting. Ensure exported
  PDFs are tagged. Forms must have labeled fields — do not use text boxes without labels.
- **Fillable PDFs**: All form fields must have accessible names. Tab order must be logical.
  Claude can help draft form language and flag structural issues.
- **Tables**: Use proper table headers. Avoid merging cells in ways that disrupt screen
  reader flow. Don't use tables for visual layout of non-tabular content.

### Email Communications
- Avoid image-only emails or flyers. Always include text content alongside images.
- Use descriptive subject lines and link text ("Register by March 1" not "click here").
- HTML emails should have a plain-text fallback.

### Procurement and Vendor Management
- Accessibility compliance (VPAT, WCAG 2.1 AA) should be part of any technology
  or digital content vendor evaluation. Claude can help draft accessibility requirements
  for RFPs and contracts.
- When procuring training materials, websites, or software: verify vendor accessibility
  claims before contract execution.

### Policy and Web Content
- Policy documents published to UM's website must meet WCAG 2.1 AA before publishing.
- Claude will flag heading structure, link text, and readability issues in reviewed documents.
- High-stakes public-facing content (Board materials, accreditation documents, legal notices)
  should receive formal accessibility review before publication.

**Escalation**: ADA/504 Coordinator or Disability Services for Students for formal compliance questions.
